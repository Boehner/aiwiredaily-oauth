AIWireDaily is a daily AI-news video brief, automatically produced and published to YouTube. Each episode summarizes that day's notable developments in artificial intelligence from public news sources.

This site exists solely to host the app information required for this channel's YouTube API integration, which uploads videos to the AIWireDaily channel on behalf of its owner.
